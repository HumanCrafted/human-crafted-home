The X-Carve Pro caps feed at 300 ipm in firmware, so the MDF and softwood rows sit at the machine limit rather than at an ideal chipload. Everything else is set by dropping spindle speed rather than pushing feed — the 8,000 rpm floor on this spindle is what makes a proper chipload reachable at all.

## Notes

Upcut geometry pulls chips up and out of the cut. That keeps deep slots clear and is the right choice for through-cuts in solid stock, but it lifts fibres at the top surface — so expect tearout on veneered sheet goods and figured hardwood unless a finishing pass cleans the edge up afterwards.

Cutting edge had dark deposits when catalogued, consistent with having been run at too low a chipload. Cleaned before measuring.
