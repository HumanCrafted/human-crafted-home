Space planning and layout concepts: how a room, shop, or home should work before anyone frames a wall or tears one out. Plans, elevations, and 3D concepts so you can see it before you commit to it. I am not a licensed architect or structural engineer, but can help you find the right help when needed.

## What a project looks like

- **Walkthrough** - how the space gets used now, and what needs to change.
- **Layout** - plans and elevations, with options to compare.
- **3D concept** - a model you can look around before you commit.
- **Handoff** - drawings to take to your contractor.
