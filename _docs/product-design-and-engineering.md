Concept through manufacturing. Industrial design and mechanical engineering from the same person, so how it looks, works, and how it gets built are decided together instead of handed back and forth.

25 years of experience in consumer products, commercial, and industrial equipment. Including innovation work for Georgia-Pacific and other global manufacturers, three Matrix commercial cardio launches, and work for Bosch Tools, Weber Grill, and Fiskars.

## What a project looks like

- **Concept** - research, sketches and models to find the right direction early.
- **Prototype** - working prototypes, tested against real use.
- **Engineering** - CAD, human factors, and the details that decide cost, reliability and assembly.
- **Manufacturing** - years of overseas manufacturing experience translated to design for manufacturing, the documentation a factory actually needs, and working with your manufacturing partners.
