## Notes

**3D finishing only, same as B01** — the center of a ball nose runs at near-zero surface speed regardless of rpm, so it's the wrong tool for slotting or clearing. Reach for [[bit-df125up|U05]], [[bit-generic-1-8-upcut|U06]], or [[bit-46127-k|U07]] for material removal at this diameter.

**Smaller radius means finer detail, not just a smaller version of B01.** A 0.0625" ball resolves contours and fillets B01's 0.125" ball can't get into — the reason to reach for this one specifically isn't size, it's the geometry it can trace.

**0.87" cutting length is generous for a 1/8" tool.** Plenty of reach for a deep 3D contour, though a longer unsupported flute also means more deflection risk on aggressive passes — keep stepover conservative on anything but a light finishing pass.
