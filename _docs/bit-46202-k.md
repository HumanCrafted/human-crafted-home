## Notes

**Maximum cut depth is 0.750".** This bit physically cannot cut through 3/4" stock — a through-cut needs the flutes to reach past the bottom face, and with a bottom offset into the spoilboard that is roughly 0.77" of travel. Reach for [[bit-420-dnc250|D01]] or [[bit-81618|U01]] for anything that goes all the way through. This one is for dados, grooves, shallow pockets, and top-face finishing passes.

**Always ramp — never plunge.** Down cut geometry augers chips downward, so a straight plunge packs the hole beneath the cutter with its own waste. Amana market this as a "plunge" bit, which refers to the end geometry being centre-cutting, not to plunging being a good idea in practice.

**Chip evacuation limits depth per pass, not cutting force.** Stepdowns above are roughly half what the same-diameter upcut takes, because chips have nowhere to go and packed chips mean heat, and heat in maple means scorch marks that cannot be sanded out at depth.
