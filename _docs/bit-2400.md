A 3/8" flush trim with a tip bearing: the bearing rides the template or the finished face below, and the flutes bring everything above it flush.

## Notes

**The bearing matches the cutting diameter**, so whatever it rides is what the cut copies. Template on the bottom, rough stock on top, one pass. The 1" cutting length covers 3/4" stock with room to reposition for a fresh section of carbide.

**This one stays out of Fusion**, and the bearing is exactly why: it's built to follow a surface by feel, which is the hand-router job. The CNC-side crossover case is the bearing-less kind, like [[bit-2052|R01]].
