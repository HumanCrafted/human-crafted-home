Small, fast sites that YOU own. Static pages, no CMS subscription, no platform deciding what your content looks like next year or how much they'll charge you. Best for simple, single or multi-page sites with limited interactivity, but [let's discuss](mailto:hello@humancrafted.co) your needs if they are more complex.

## What a project looks like

- **Design** - layout, type, and color built as a small system, not a set of one-off pages. I can [[brand-and-identity-design|build a new identity]], or faithfully re-create what you already have.
- **Build** - static site (Astro, Jekyll, or similar), responsive and fast, light and dark modes
- **Content workflow** - write in markdown or publish using a cloud-based CMS admin (Keystatic or similar)
- **Deploy** - I can host (GitHub Pages or Vercel) with a domain and analytics wired up, or I can hand over everything and walk you through deploying on your own.
- **Handoff** - the design and code is yours, documented well enough to keep going without me, but I'll never leave you hanging.

If you don't know what any of this means, don't worry. It's not complicated and I'll guide you through all of it.
