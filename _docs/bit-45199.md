The odd one out next to [[bit-46200|D03]] and [[bit-46341|D04]]: same 1/8" cutting diameter, but straight flutes instead of a spiral, and a straight-through 1/8" shank instead of their reduced 1/4". [[bit-45525|S01]]'s little sibling in geometry class, not its little sibling in size — S01 is a 1-1/8" surfacing head, this is a precision detail bit two orders of magnitude smaller.

For the X-Carve Pro and its 1.5 kW spindle. ## Notes

**This one can plunge straight down, unlike every downcut in the drawer.** A downcut spiral augers chips into its own hole on a plunge, which is why D01–D04 are all ramp-only. A straight flute has no helix to do that — it shears in place without pulling chips vertically, so a direct Z plunge doesn't pack the hole. Amana grinds this bit center-cutting specifically for that reason.

**Maximum cut depth is 0.4375"** — the shallowest bit in the shop. Built for detail work: engraving-adjacent passes, fine pocket floors, anything where finish quality matters more than material removal rate.

**No reduced shank.** D03 and D04 step down from a 1/4" shank to a 1/8" cutting edge; this one is 1/8" all the way from collet to tip. Less rigid over the exposed length as a result — light passes aren't optional here the way they're merely prudent on the reduced-shank bits.
