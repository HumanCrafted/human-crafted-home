**Lubricate in aluminum.** Same rule as the other metal bits: aluminum galls onto a dry cutter, coated or not. WD-40 or A9 and clear the chips.

**No wood presets, on purpose.** [[bit-46379|B01]] is the wood ballnose and cuts cleaner there; a preset that exists will eventually get picked.

## Notes

**Fiberglass and G10 are respirator jobs.** Dust extraction on, respirator on, same as with [[bit-8515a227|U02]]. The dust is abrasive glass, not sawdust.

**Center cutting, so it can plunge** — but at 15 ipm in metal, a ramp entry is still gentler on the tip. The ball's center has near-zero surface speed, same as every ball and V tool in the drawer.

**1.125" of flute is the most reach of any 1/4" bit here**, worth remembering when a 3D contour dips into a deep pocket the shorter bits can't finish.
