For the X-Carve Pro and its 1.5 kW spindle. These match the presets stored on this tool in the Fusion hub library. Stepover is the 3D-finishing pitch between parallel passes.

The chipload column is the full-slot number; in a finishing pass the ball is barely engaged and the real chip is far thinner, which is why these sit at higher RPM than [[bit-81618|U01]] without rubbing. The tip itself has near-zero rim speed, same story as [[bit-45704|V01]].

## Notes

**This is a finishing tool.** For 2D slotting or profiling at full depth, [[bit-81618|U01]] does it better — square corners, proper chipload at 14,000. The presets here assume Parallel or 3D Contour passes over a roughed shape.

**Stepover sets the scallop.** At 0.025" between passes a 1/4" ball leaves ridges about 0.0006" tall — sandpaper territory. Halving the stepover quarters the scallop and doubles the runtime; going coarser shows tool marks fast.

**Rough first with U01**, leaving 0.02–0.03" of stock. The ball then cuts a consistent thin skin everywhere instead of alternating between air and full slots.

**The mirror-finish grind is aimed at plastics.** Amana sells this line for acrylic and HDPE surface work; the acrylic preset is its home turf, not an afterthought.
