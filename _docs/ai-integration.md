I utilize AI tools across all aspects of my own business: research, advice, planning, records, documentation, interfacing with software tools, coding, and running daily automations. If you're wondering where AI could help your business, I can help you find out and get it working.

## What a project looks like

- **Working session** - half a day on how your business runs and where AI could fit.
- **Integration** - from building skills and setting up environments, to connecting to the tools you already use.
- **Mentoring** - working with you or your people on repeatable systems.

## Good fit for

Any size business or community group that wants AI doing real work:

- **Working inside your tools** - accounting, email, calendars, CRM, even design and CAD software.
- **Running your processes** - a multi-step job turned into a skill anyone on the team can run, done your way every time.
- **Knowing your business** - answers from your own documents, past jobs and manuals.
- **Drafting in your voice** - quotes, proposals, reports and marketing, based on how you've done them before.
- **Reading your numbers** - plain questions about sales, job costs or inventory.
- **Working on a schedule** - morning briefings, monitoring and follow-ups that run without anyone starting them.
- **Research** - markets, competitors, vendors and regulations.
