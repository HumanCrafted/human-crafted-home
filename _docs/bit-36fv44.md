## Notes

**3/16" cut depth is the whole story with this line.** MK Morse calls this their "Shallow" CT Hole Cutter, built for fabrication work in sheet material, not for boring through anything thick. Grainger's own listing claims 1/8" max cutting depth, which contradicts MK Morse's own site (3/16") and what's printed on the packaging — trusting the manufacturer and the physical bit over the retailer's listing here.

**Built-in arbor means no separate arbor to lose.** Unlike a classic hole saw that threads onto a reusable arbor, this cutter and its arbor are one piece — simpler, but the whole cutter goes in the toolbox together rather than an arbor living separately from a set of interchangeable saws.

**Step-center pilot reduces breakthrough impact.** The pilot drill (1/4" dia, 1-7/8" long) leads the cut and controls when the carbide teeth actually engage, which is what keeps a carbide tooth from chipping the instant it breaks through the back of thin stock.

**Vendor here is Grainger, not MK Morse directly** — Grainger's item number (36FV44) is what's on hand, cross-referenced to MK Morse's own CTS14 model number for anyone reordering direct from the manufacturer instead.
