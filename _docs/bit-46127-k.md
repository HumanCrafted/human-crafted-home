## Notes

**Plunge-ground, like U04.** Amana markets the Spektra spiral-plunge line specifically for direct Z entry, and being an upcut it doesn't have the chip-packing problem a downcut would.

**Tears the top face on plywood**, same tradeoff as every upcut in the shop. Reach for a downcut when the top surface shows.

**Spektra earns its keep on anything abrasive** — MDF and laminate especially, per the same reasoning as [[bit-46202-k|D02]] and [[bit-46248-k|U04]]. For plain hardwood the coating doesn't cost anything but doesn't buy much either; [[bit-generic-1-8-upcut|U06]] or [[bit-df125up|U05]] do the same job there for less.
