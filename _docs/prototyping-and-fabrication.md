Working prototypes and short production runs, made in the studio, plus the fixturing and patterns a run needs when it has to repeat.

## What I can make

| Process | Machine | Materials |
|---|---|---|
| [Laser cutting](/laser-cutter/) | 1300 x 900 mm, 150 W CO2 | Wood, acrylic, cardboard, fabric, leather, paper |
| [CNC routing](/cnc-router/) | X-Carve Pro, 4 x 2 ft | Wood, plywood, MDF, acrylic, foam |
| [3D printing](/3d-printer/) | Bambu X2D, Prusa MINI+ | PLA, PETG, HTPLA |

I don't do metal cutting, welding, injection molding or large production runs, but I can connect you to someone who does or facilitate it as part of a project.
