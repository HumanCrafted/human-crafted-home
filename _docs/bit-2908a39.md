## Notes

**Same line as T01, one size up.** Short length for rigidity, TiN for the metal work it'll probably never see but doesn't cost anything in wood or plastic either. Reach for this one over T01 whenever 3/16" is the actual target size rather than 1/8".

**Two 3/16" bits in the drawer now, for different reasons.** [[bit-3045a21|T02]] is the sheet-metal specialist: short flutes, black oxide, built to clear thin stock without snagging. This one is the general-purpose short-length twist. Sharpie makes the call, not the diameter.

**0.84" max drilling depth** is enough for through-holes in 3/4" stock with a little room to spare, more headroom than T01 has at 1/8".
