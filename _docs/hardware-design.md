Hole and pocket sizes for metric hardware in 3D-printed parts, M3 through M10, in millimeters. Press fit is for nuts that stay put; slip fit is for nuts and bolts that need to drop in. Based initially on the [hardware design boards](https://www.alch.shop/projects/p/hardware-design-boards) from alch.shop. Hardware sizes and offsets managed in a hardware.yaml file.

{% include hardware-chart.html %}

### Bolts, screws, and nuts

Hex head bolts are zinc-plated Class 8.8 steel (DIN 933), socket head cap screws and flat head screws are black-oxide alloy steel (DIN 912 and DIN 7991), and hex nuts are zinc-plated Class 8 steel (DIN 934), all from McMaster-Carr.
