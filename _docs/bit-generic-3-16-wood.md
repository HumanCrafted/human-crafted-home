## Notes

**Unidentified, unlike everything else in the T series.** No stamped brand or part number to look up, no McMaster page to confirm coating or steel grade against — the size and lengths here are read straight off the bit itself, not a vendor spec sheet. If a manufacturer mark turns up later, update this note.

**Brad point, so it's the wood specialist of the 3/16" bits.** The center spur locates the hole and the two outer spurs score the fiber ahead of the flutes, so the entry stays clean instead of tearing out — [[bit-3045a21|T02]] and [[bit-2908a39|T04]] are both twist geometry, built for metal first and wood as an afterthought. Reach for this one whenever the hole itself needs to look good, not just exist.

**Deepest reach in the drawer, too.** 2" of drilling depth clears every other 3/16" bit here (T02 at 0.75", T04 at 0.84") by a wide margin — dowel joinery in thick stock especially.
