## Other Notes
Single origin EA decaf from Guji, Ethiopia. 12 oz (340 g) whole bean. Roasted Aug 12, 2026 (confirmed). Bag photo is Ceremony's stock decaf render — same bag design, but the label reads "Decaf Colombia" (Ceremony reuses one rotating decaf render).

It's way darker than I like, and not what I would call medium roast.  Filter was ok / fine, but I got really good espresso pulls from this coffee.
