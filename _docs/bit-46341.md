Same size as [[bit-46200|D03]] down to the thousandth: 1/8" cutting diameter, 1/2" cutting length, 1/4" reduced shank, 2" overall. Different bit, though. Amana grinds this one with a steeper shear angle specifically for solid hardwood, where D03 is the general-purpose version that also handles composites and sheet goods.

## Feeds & Speeds

For the X-Carve Pro and its 1.5 kW spindle. Amana rates this bit for 20,000–21,000 rpm, meaningfully higher than D03's 14,000–16,000, so the wood presets below run faster across the board — same halved stepdown as D03 though, since that's a function of the 1/8" shaft's rigidity, not the flute grind.

## Notes

**Maximum cut depth is 0.500"**, same limit as [[bit-46200|D03]] — shallow work only, nothing through 3/4" stock.

**Ramp only here too, despite Amana's own marketing.** Amana's copy for this bit specifically claims "fast plunge action," but that's describing the center-cutting tip's ability to plunge axially, not a green light to ignore the chip-packing problem every downcut spiral has. The auger-downward geometry that makes this bit shear so cleanly is exactly what packs a straight plunge full of its own chips. House rule stands: ramp, don't plunge.

**Run this one over D03 whenever the material is solid hardwood** and the finish matters — that's the entire reason Amana ground it differently. Reach for D03 instead on plywood, MDF, or anything that isn't solid lumber; its more general-purpose grind doesn't leave performance on the table there the way this one might.

**Same reduced-shank caveat as D03**: Fusion's flat-end-mill type won't visualize the neck taper between the 1/8" flutes and 1/4" shank, but the shank diameter itself stores correctly for collet clearance.
