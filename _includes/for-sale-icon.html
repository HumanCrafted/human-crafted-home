{%- comment -%} "Available to buy" marker on an archive card: an ink disc with a paper "$". A text glyph rather than an SVG icon — at 18px a stroked bag reads as mush, while a "$" stays legible because it's a real letterform being rendered at a size the typeface was drawn for. Rendered when a project has `for_sale: true`. The caller must place this INSIDE a `.title-tail` (white-space: nowrap) span together with the title's last word — see index.md. The disc is an atomic inline box, so the browser will push it onto a line of its own when the title fills its column (the "$" stranded alone under a mobile card). A word joiner does NOT prevent that; only making the last word and the disc one unbreakable inline does. {%- endcomment -%} $ Available to buy
