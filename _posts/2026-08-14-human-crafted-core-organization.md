  ![[new-3d-printer.jpeg|w=500]]
- I've made a few new updates to the [Human Crafted wiki](https://humancrafted.co/re/) on my site which you can reach by clicking on the three-lines icon at the top of the homepage.  This wiki is something I am adding to over time, and hope it can be a helpful resource to anyone out there.  Many of the pages and notes are really just a resource for me that I wanted documented, but instead of bookmarks in a browser or notes on a drive, I thought it would be more useful to publish them on my site. 
   ![[human-crafted-core.png|w=500]]
- A little aside about the wiki name, co/re... For a while now I have had the .co ending to my url, and thought it was clever to use it as part of my contact page I call co/lab (like the slash is one of the two L's).  Co/re is an extension of that.  This wiki is meant to document what I consider the "core" of human crafted, the things that make it the identity it is.  Also, the re conveniently is short for "regarding" so you can read it that way too... re: Music, re: Coffee Beans, re: Process, or re: Human Crafted.
- One new entry to co/re I wanted to call out separately is the [places](https://humancrafted.co/places/) map. I had Claude help me research and add all of the [Wisconsin Foodie](https://www.wisconsinfoodie.com) spots from the last 10 seasons as well as all of the [Top Chef](https://www.bravotv.com/top-chef) contestant restaurants. These are both resources I look at when researching new places to visit and want to find good restaurants. Now it's available to anyone. I will be adding other resources like these to the Places map over time so check back often and let me know what else you'd find helpful to see on this map.
