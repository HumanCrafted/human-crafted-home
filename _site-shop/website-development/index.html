Website Development

Small, fast sites that YOU own. Static pages, no CMS subscription, no platform deciding what your content looks like next year or how much they’ll charge you. Best for simple, single or multi-page sites with limited interactivity, but let’s discuss your needs if they are more complex.

What a project looks like

  • Design - layout, type, and color built as a small system, not a set of one-off pages. I can build a new identity, or faithfully re-create what you already have.
  • Build - static site (Astro, Jekyll, or similar), responsive and fast, light and dark modes
  • Content workflow - write in markdown or publish using a cloud-based CMS admin (Keystatic or similar)
  • Deploy - I can host (GitHub Pages or Vercel) with a domain and analytics wired up, or I can hand over everything and walk you through deploying on your own.
  • Handoff - the design and code is yours, documented well enough to keep going without me, but I’ll never leave you hanging.

Good fit for

Studio and portfolio sites, non-profits, product one-pagers, documentation, and small business sites that need to stay fast and stay cheap to run. If you need e-commerce let’s discuss.

What it costs

Cost depends on page count and how much design work is net-new versus adapting something you already have. Hosting is typically free or a few dollars a month. Full projects range from a few hundred to a few thousand. Get in touch and let’s make something great.
