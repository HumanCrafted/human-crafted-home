About me

Hi, I’m Jon, a creative engineer. I started Human Crafted in 2016 as a boutique design studio and product design brand after having worked across agencies and corporate OEM’s for the previous 15 years. I specialize in rapidly building solutions to test, informing future steps, and above all… making progress.

Setting sail as an entrepreneur and freelance designer wasn’t an easy decision, but taking the path less traveled has become a trend that I’ve learned to embrace. Throughout this site you will find a diversified range of projects and products, each starting with an idea to make something better at its core. This pursuit of better has driven me to fine tune my craft for speed and efficiency, developing expertise that spans creative design and technical engineering so I can adapt to a new need at any moment. I coined the term “Creative Engineer” to describe this unique combination. I am proud of this wide range, and I’ve come to appreciate it as my super power. 🦸🏻‍♂️
