## What I build

**A website.** Up to five pages, your words and photos, on your own domain. Nothing to subscribe to. $1,500 to $3,000.

**A tool.** One job that lives on paper, in a spreadsheet, or in a group text today: a sign-up, a directory, a map, a form that goes somewhere, a schedule, a roster. $2,500 to $6,000.

**Keeping it running.** Hosting, the domain, updates, and small changes when you need them. $50 to $150 a month.

The price is fixed before the work starts. Most builds take days, not months.

## How it goes

1. We talk for half an hour about what you need.
2. I send a one-page scope and price.
3. I build it, you check it, it goes live.
