The Bath Container is a small acrylic cube with a solid maple lid, sized for cotton swabs, cotton balls, and the other little things that end up loose on a bathroom counter. It was made to sit next to the [[tissue-dispenser|Tissue Dispenser]], in the same colors with the same maple on top.

First made in November 2019, sold in markets and online.
