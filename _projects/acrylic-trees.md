The Acrylic Trees are small laser-cut trees. Each one is two flat pieces of acrylic, slotted halfway so they cross and stand on their own. Mix colors and sizes and you've got a mini forest for a shelf or mantel.

They started in 2016 as a holiday decoration on my table at the Good Day Market. Enough people asked about them that I made more, first as a set of three cut from scrap acrylic, then, as the years went by, in more sizes and colors, with palettes put together with help from [@clairetommcolor](https://www.instagram.com/clairetommcolor/).

After a few years off, they came back in 2025 with [Good Day Shop](https://www.instagram.com/gooddayshop__/): cleaned-up packaging, a new lineup of sizes, and lots of new colors. They sold well, so this year there's another lineup at Good Day Shop, and now for the first time in a long time, the trees are on sale here too!

### Sizes and colors

Five sizes, all cut from 1/8" acrylic.
