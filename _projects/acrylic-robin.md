The Acrylic Robin is a laser-cut robin, my home state bird, and shamelessly inspired by the [Eames House Bird](https://www.eamesoffice.com/product/eames-house-bird/?gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=12558519040&gbraid=0AAAAADnH7C2wQ7TBrvL4kK-A6DB8Yxilw&gclid=CjwKCAjwsrbTBhAvEiwA0Bpp4SEY9nfQbsPV4coVCAQ7UkPkZq0fKJIbfIQHhh30HzNt06sajYJ47BoCh0EQAvD_BwE) (c. 1940s). A flat acrylic profile of a robin, standing on two thin legs. Set it on a shelf or a windowsill. The real robins come and go with the season here in Wisconsin. This one stays.

### Making it

The body is a single piece of acrylic, cut on the laser.. Two thin legs slide into the bottom edge so it stands on its own.
